Savvy Coming soon to iPhone

Stop overpaying for the same stuff.

Savvy is a free iPhone app. Scan a barcode and see the cheapest places to buy that exact product, alongside your preferred store’s price for comparison. Copy coupon codes in one tap, and keep a monthly savings meter of the listed gaps you find.

What it does

01

Scan a barcode

Point your camera at a product, or type the code if you need to. Savvy looks up that exact item.

02

See the cheapest places

Compare last-seen prices for the same product, including the price at your preferred store.

03

Copy coupon codes

Store coupon codes are ready in one tap, so you can paste them at checkout.

04

Monthly savings meter

A simple meter on your phone that records the listed price gaps you find each month.

Savvy may earn a commission when you buy through links in the app, at no extra cost to you.
